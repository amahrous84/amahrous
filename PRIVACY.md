# Privacy Policy: Unreal But True upload tool

This tool is used only by the owner of the YouTube channel "Unreal But True" to upload that channel's own videos and thumbnails and to read its statistics.

- It signs in with Google only to access the owner's own YouTube channel.
- It does not collect, store, sell or share data about any other person.
- Sign-in tokens are kept privately by the owner (on the owner's computer and in private GitHub repository secrets) and are never published.
- Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: buttrueunreal@gmail.com
