## The one fix: give people a reason to subscribe
Every Short is a stand-alone "What If...?" with a generic SUBSCRIBE button. Nothing tells the
viewer there is a new one every day, so a viewer who liked it has no reason to come back.
This week: number the series and promise the next one.
- Title stays plain; on-screen opening badge "WHAT IF #N".
- Last line before the question: "Tomorrow: what if <next topic>?" (a real, queued topic).
- Description first line: "A new what-if every day at 5:48 pm (Riyadh)."
- Pin a comment on each Short with tomorrow's topic.

Prerequisite: uploads stopped after Oct 24 (fal.ai out of credit). A promise of "tomorrow"
only works if tomorrow's Short actually comes.

## Already working (keep)
1. The lane: space/physics what-ifs. 6 of the last 7 got 900-1,570 views; off-lane Shorts got under 400.
2. The moving opening: Jupiter Disappeared kept 60% of viewers past the start, the best on the channel.
3. The yes/no debate ending: Jupiter Disappeared got 4 comments; Shorts before it had 0.
